Arte

The site is a canvas of what was, what is, and what is coming.

永 (Eternity)

13 November 2026 · Gold brushwork on black

永 is a symbol for eternity. A body swimming in a river: the river flows, time flows. What was, what is, and what is coming move in the same current, and the body is set free in it.

Related work · It Feels Me / Me siente

Hunger

13 November 2026 · Gold brushwork on black, after the artist’s drawing

A mother on her knees, a child reaching, and the earth already in that small hand. The dark around them is full of stars. I drew what the body knows before it has a name for hunger.

Related work · The Oldest Hunger / El hambre más antigua

望 (Wàng) / Wish

13 November 2026 · Gold brushwork on black, after the artist’s drawing

He looks up to the stars. He wishes, he wonders. He calls out for answers. Now he takes it as his own.

Mira hacia las estrellas. Desea, se pregunta. Clama por respuestas. Ahora lo toma como suyo.
