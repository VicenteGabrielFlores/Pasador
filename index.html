The Harness-Maker’s Son

Book One · 1706–1731 · about 34,000 words

The book underneath the others. In 1706 a mountain comes down on the harbor of Garachico. In 1731, at the fork of two rivers in Texas, the villa of San Fernando is founded. San Antonio de Béxar dates from 1718. Told as a novel in dramatic-script form, with the Spanish left untranslated.

PASADOR

1731–1836 Béxar, the Alamo, and Gonzales, through 1913 Juárez and El Paso · 57,826 words

A Tejano family saga told as five plays in the bilingual register of the border. It opens on the night of November 15, 1913, when José Jesús Flores Stephenson presses a folded paper into his ten-year-old son Carlos’s hand and gives him one word: Sigue. The five plays are The Canary, The Revolution, The River, The Mine, and The Mayor.

CONCORDIA: la heredera

1828–1870 · Juana María Ascárate · in development

The prose novel of Juana María Ascárate, heir of Concordia. Her documented dates are 8 February 1809 to 6 February 1856. The novel’s years run from the 1828 marriage at El Paso del Norte through 1870. No word count is published yet.

Belmont Road

A documentary-verse play. An eighteen-year-old walks home from a football social and does not arrive. What England does with the space afterward is the play. It does not depict the killing and does not enter the courtroom.
