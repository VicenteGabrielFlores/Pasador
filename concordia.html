CONCORDIA: la heredera

1828–1870 · Juana María Ascárate

Vicente Gabriel Flores

A prose novel in development. Its years are 1828 to 1870, the Concordia years of Juana María Ascárate and the house that took her name. On this site she is documented as born 8 February 1809 and died 6 February 1856. She built the first Catholic church on the east bank of the Río Grande, San José de Concordia el Alto, in 1854. She was the first person buried in what became Concordia Cemetery.

No word count and no ISBN are recorded here. What the novel will invent is not yet the page. What the papers say is on the other shelf.

The Girl at Concordia is fiction from PASADOR, set in the autumn of 1848. The twelve-year-old on the acequia bank is Margarita, Juana’s daughter. It is not this novel.
